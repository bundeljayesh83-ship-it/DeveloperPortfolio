## Binary Number Representation

### Decimal to Binary Conversion (75)

75 ÷ 2 = 37 remainder 1
37 ÷ 2 = 18 remainder 1
18 ÷ 2 = 9 remainder 0
9 ÷ 2 = 4 remainder 1
4 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 0
2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0
1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1

Reading remainders from bottom to top:

75 = (1001011)₂

### Binary to Decimal Conversion (110101)

(110101)₂

= 1 × 2⁵ + 1 × 2⁴ + 0 × 2³
+ 1 × 2² + 0 × 2¹ + 1 × 2⁰

= 32 + 16 + 0 + 4 + 0 + 1

= 53

### Why Computers Use Binary

Computers use binary because electronic circuits
can represent two states: ON (1) and OFF (0).
